TF-IDF calculator

One document per line. Uses the same convention as the Unit 2 numericals: TF = count / words, IDF = log₁₀(N / DF).

Bigram probability calculator

One sentence per line. Each sentence is wrapped in <s> and </s>. V counts the distinct tokens including the two boundary markers.

Press Compute. (reading → books gives 1/2; reading → movies is unseen, so Laplace gives 1/(2+V).)

The answer to "design a preprocessing pipeline"

Preprocessing recipe

  1. Clean: remove HTML, URLs, odd symbols; normalise unicode and spacing.
  2. Lowercase (but keep case if it matters, for example for named entities).
  3. Tokenize: split into words or sub-words.
  4. Remove stop words and punctuation (skip for tasks that need them, such as sentiment with negation).
  5. Normalise: stemming (fast, rough, "quickly" gives "quickli") or lemmatization (dictionary and POS, "running" gives "run").
  6. Optionally tag: POS tags, named entities, parse.
  7. Vectorise: BoW or TF-IDF for classic models, embeddings for neural models.

Justify each step by the task: preprocessing that helps topic classification can hurt sentiment analysis.
