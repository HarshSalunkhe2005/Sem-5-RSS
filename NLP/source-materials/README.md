# NLP Source Materials

Raw source decks pulled from the Moodle course room (`Natural Language Processing_26-27`),
kept here for reference/provenance. The built notes in `NLP/Unit 2` and `NLP/Unit 3` are the
self-contained, actually-useful pages — treat this folder as an archive, not something to read
directly.

All files below are from the course instructor (Suman Tanwar) — other divisions' experiential
learning materials and the general CO-evaluation folders were skipped as out of scope.

- **Unit-2-Feature-Extraction-Language-Modeling.pdf** — main Unit 2 deck: One-Hot, BoW, N-Grams,
  CountVectorizer, TF-IDF, probabilistic language modeling, Naive Bayes, generative models overview.
- **Unit-3-Word-Embeddings.pdf** — main Unit 3 deck: Word2Vec (CBOW/Skip-gram), Gensim, GloVe.
- **Unit-2-Practice-Questions-Solutions.pdf** — 40 application/numerical Unit 2 questions with
  worked solutions ("Practice Worksheet of UNIT-2").
- **All-Units-Application-Worksheet.pdf** — 18 scenario-based questions across One-Hot, CBOW/Skip-gram,
  TF-IDF, BoW, GloVe, and word embeddings generally ("Practice Worksheet of all UNITS").
- **Official-Question-Bank-CO.pdf** — the full 5-unit official CO-tagged question bank; only the
  Unit 2 (CO2) and Unit 3 (CO3) sections were used here.
- **Practice-Questions.pptx** / **Practice-Answers.pptx** — a 31-question general Q&A set (MCQ/TF/
  fill-in-blank/numerical/conceptual) spanning Unit 2 and Unit 3 topics together.
