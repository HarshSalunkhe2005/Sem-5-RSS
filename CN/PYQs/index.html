Q3You applied cyclic redundancy check (CRC) using the dataword 1001 and the divisor 1011, and generated a codeword. However, during transmission, the received codeword is 1001110. Does the received codeword contain any errors?6 marks

Divide the received codeword 1001110 by the divisor 1011 using modulo-2 (XOR) division. A non-zero remainder means the codeword contains a transmission error and should be rejected.
