CRC calculator

Sender: enter the dataword and divisor to get the codeword. Receiver: enter the received codeword and the divisor to check for an error (Dec 2024 paper: divisor 1011, received 1001110).

Worked answer to that question: the sender's dataword 1001 with divisor 1011 gives remainder 110, so the correct codeword is 1001110. The received word is exactly that, and dividing it by 1011 leaves remainder 000, so no error is detected.
